Core idea

Reality is an
informational field.

Every system — cognitive, social, physical — can be described as a distinguishability field Σ with a density ρ. The fundamental question is not "what is happening" but "how is density changing."

01
Emotion is ∂ρ/∂t
Emotion is not a label. It is the registered experience of your field compressing or expanding — the sign and magnitude of density change over time.
02
Attention is conductance
The attention economy has mistaken capture for influence. What matters is not whether information arrives — but whether it passes through at all.
03
Repetition builds resistance
Every repeated pattern that arrives before value is experienced adds structural resistance S. This is why the same message stops working — the structure is blocked, not the message.
04
One formalism, all scales
The same operators O₀ (repeat) and O₁ (new distinction) govern individual cognition, social systems, and physical field dynamics. The language is unified.
